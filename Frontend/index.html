About Zoomies

The Choice That Moves You

Zoomies is a curated performance marketplace built for drivers who care about what a car feels like, not only what it costs. We connect trusted sellers with buyers looking for clean, exciting, pre-owned vehicles.

Our Story

Founded by a small team of car people, Zoomies started as a simpler way to compare enthusiast listings without digging through noisy classifieds. The goal is to keep the buying process focused, transparent, and fast.

What We Do

We bring together listings, wishlists, seller messaging, finance estimates, and fair-price tools so shoppers can move from discovery to decision with fewer tabs, fewer guesses, and better context.
